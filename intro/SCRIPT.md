# Foodeiblog – 20 Second Motion Graphic Intro

**Format:** 1920×1080 / 16:9 · 20 seconds · warm cream + orange palette (taken from the logo)
**Files:** `index.html` (the animation), `logo.png` (swap for your own logo), `foodeiblog-intro.webm` (recorded video)

---

## Shot-by-shot script

| Time | Visual (what's on screen) | Motion | Voiceover (VO) | Sound |
|------|---------------------------|--------|----------------|-------|
| **0:00 – 0:03** | Soft cream background. A warm orange glow blooms from the centre. Tiny orange "spice" dots float up from the bottom. | A thin orange line draws out from the centre, then fades. | *(silence / music only)* | Soft whoosh + light music starts (acoustic guitar or lo-fi beat) |
| **0:03 – 0:05** | Big serif word **TASTE.** | Drops in from blurred and wide-spaced → snaps sharp → floats up and out. | "Taste…" | Beat hit on the word |
| **0:05 – 0:06** | **COOK.** | Same kinetic move. | "Cook…" | Beat hit / sizzle sound |
| **0:06 – 0:08** | **SHARE.** | Same kinetic move, held slightly longer. | "Share." | Beat hit |
| **0:08 – 0:10** | A plate appears: orange outer ring and thin inner ring. | Plate spins in (-90° → 0°) while both rings draw themselves. | *(music)* | Soft "ding" of a plate |
| **0:09 – 0:12** | Fork slides in from the left, spoon from the right. Steam curls rise above the plate. | Slide-in with ease-out; steam loops upward. | *(music)* | Gentle steam hiss |
| **0:12 – 0:14** | **Foodeiblog logo** is revealed in the centre of the plate. | Left-to-right wipe, then a small bounce. | "Welcome to Foodeiblog." | Music swell / riser |
| **0:14 – 0:16** | Logo holds on screen. | A white light shine sweeps across the logo. | *(music)* | Sparkle "shing" |
| **0:16 – 0:19** | Tagline *"Recipes, stories & flavours from every kitchen"* and **FOODEIBLOG.COM** below. | Both rise up and fade in, one after the other. | "Recipes, stories and flavours from every kitchen." | Music continues |
| **0:19 – 0:20** | Everything fades to cream. | Logo scales up slightly and fades; screen fades out. | *(silence)* | Final soft hit, music out |

## Voiceover script (read-through, ~20 sec)

> *(0:03)* Taste… *(0:05)* Cook… *(0:06)* Share.
> *(0:12)* Welcome to **Foodeiblog**.
> *(0:16)* Recipes, stories and flavours from every kitchen.

Tone: warm, friendly, slightly smiling. Leave pauses between the three words so they land on the beats.

---

## How to use / edit

1. **Preview:** open `intro/index.html` in Chrome. Click **↻ Replay** (bottom right) to play it again.
2. **Change the logo:** replace `intro/logo.png` with your logo (transparent PNG, wide shape works best). No code change needed.
3. **Change text:** edit the `TASTE / COOK / SHARE`, tagline and website lines near the bottom of `index.html`.
4. **Change colours:** edit `--orange`, `--cream` and `--deep` at the top of the `<style>` block.
5. **Export a video:** open `index.html?clean` (hides the replay button) full screen and screen-record for 20 s,
   or use the ready-made `foodeiblog-intro.webm`. Add music and the voiceover in any editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci).
