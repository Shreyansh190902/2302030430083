# Balaji × Haldiram — "Your competitor is not your enemy" (English)

9 slides, 1080×1440, red/black/white poster style, Hind Vadodara font. Built from the Chandubhai
Virani reel points plus public accounts of the story (Haldiram's Noida factory fire; Balaji
manufacturing for Haldiram's for two years; "Ravan / Ram" quote).

- `slides.html` — source; `out/`, `out-jpg/`, `balaji-haldiram-carousel-jpg.zip` — exports
- Re-render: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node render.js slides.html out`
