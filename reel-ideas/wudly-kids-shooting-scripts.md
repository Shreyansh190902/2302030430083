# Wudly – Full Shooting Scripts (with kids)
Each script: scene · camera · direction for child/parent · dialogue / voice-over (VO) · on-screen text · sound.
VO can be recorded later by a parent or team member. Keep kids' lines natural – if they say it differently, keep their version.

---

## REEL 1 – Crack Code: "Brother vs Sister" (15 sec · kids 10–12)

## Crew & kit checklist
- 1 camera person, 1 helper (props/timer), 1 parent per child
- Phone/camera + small tripod + gimbal (for walker tracking shot)
- 1 reflector, extension-free setup (no floor cables)
- Snacks, wipes, spare clothes, a few backup toys
- Signed parent consent forms
