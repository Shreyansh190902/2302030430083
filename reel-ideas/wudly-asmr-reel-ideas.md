## Filming tips
- **Phone** is enough: hold the phone 20–30 cm from the toy, mic close; switch off fan/AC.
- **Surface:** wooden table or cream cloth; Wudly red wall as background for brand look.
- **Light:** soft daylight from a window, no harsh shadows.
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 4K/60fps if possible (slow-motion works great for beads & tops).
- **Edit:** first 1 second must already have a sound; no music in the first 3 sec; add Wudly logo top-right + "wudlytoys.com" end card (I can add these).
- **Caption hook ideas:** "Turn your sound on 🔊", "Which sound is your favourite?", "Tag a parent who needs this calm".
- **Hashtags:** #WudlyToys #ASMR #WoodenToys #SatisfyingSounds #PlayNaturally

## Posting plan (suggested)
2 ASMR Reels a week: Mon = one-toy close-up, Thu = satisfying build/knock-down.
