# Wudly – Reel Ideas for New Products (beyond ASMR)

Hands-only / product-only (no kids' faces). 10–30 sec, vertical 9:16.

## Weather Sort
1. **"What's the weather today?" daily routine** – parent hand sets today's weather each morning; series for a week.
2. **Learn & say** – text pops: Sunny ☀️ / Rainy 🌧️ / Snowy ❄️ as each piece is placed.
3. **Monsoon trend** – real rain outside the window, then the rain piece goes on the board.

## My Farm House
1. **"Who lives here?" guessing game** – animal hidden behind the barn door, viewers guess in comments.
2. **Animal sounds challenge** – each animal placed with a voice-over "moo / baa / quack".
3. **Story time in 15 sec** – mini story: "Morning on the farm…" told with the toys.

## My Sorting Dino
1. **"Feed the hungry dino"** – POV the dino eats shapes; text "Only circles today!"
2. **Colour & shape quiz** – "Which shape fits next?" pause → reveal.
3. **Dino vs mess** – toys scattered → all sorted into the dino (tidy-up hack).

## Forest Fun
1. **Build a forest timelapse** – empty table → full forest scene.
2. **Spot the animal** – hide one animal among the trees, "Can you find the fox?"
3. **Nature talk** – facts pop up: "Owls sleep in the day 🦉".

## Alpho Spell (English / Hindi)
1. **Spell your child's name** – "Comment a name, we'll spell it!" (reply with videos = engagement).
2. **English vs Hindi** – same word in both sets side by side.
3. **3-letter word challenge** – C-A-T, D-O-G, speed-up each word.

## Recycle Ride
1. **Eco lesson** – "Paper, plastic or glass?" sort into the right bin.
2. **World Environment Day / Earth Day post** – "Teach recycling early ♻️".
3. **Race to the bin** – timer on screen, how fast can it be sorted?

## Spin Alpha
1. **Spin a word** – spin wheels until a word appears, viewers guess the word.
2. **Alphabet song sync** – spin to the beat of a phonics song.
3. **"First letter of your name"** – spin & stop, "Did it land on yours?"

## Number Orbit
1. **Count with me 1–10** – each number moves around the orbit with a counting voice-over.
2. **Space theme** – planets 🪐 sticker overlay, "Numbers in space".
3. **Maths mini-quiz** – "3 + 2 = ?" then move the answer into place.

## Little Artist
1. **Fast-motion art** – blank → finished drawing in 10 sec.
2. **"Rate my art 1–10"** – funny caption, invites comments.
3. **Screen-free Sunday** – art set as the alternative to a tablet.

## Spinomoji
1. **"How are you feeling today?" poll Reel** – spin and stop on an emotion, ask followers.
2. **Emotion lesson for parents** – "Name the feeling, calm the feeling" tip.
3. **Mood of the week** – Monday 😴 / Friday 😄 relatable meme.

## Equalizer
1. **Balance challenge** – "Will it tip?" suspense, add one more piece.
2. **Maths made fun** – 2 + 3 = 5 balanced on the scale.
3. **Parent vs toy** – "Even adults can't do this first try" challenge.

## Rollimals
1. **Animal race** – line up and roll, viewers pick a winner before.
2. **Ramp build** – make a ramp with books, roll the animals down.
3. **Slow-mo roll** – cute close-up of each animal rolling past.

---

## Formats that work for every product
- **Top 5 / Top 3** – "Top 3 toys for 3-year-olds" featuring these products.
- **This or That** – two products side by side: "Weather Sort or Spinomoji?"
- **Unboxing** – box opening + first look.
- **How to play in 15 sec** – quick tutorial with text steps.
- **Parent tip Reels** – "1 toy, 5 ways to play" (e.g. Farm House: count, colours, sounds, story, tidy-up).
- **Gift idea Reels** – birthday, Diwali, Children's Day.
- **Comparison** – wooden vs plastic, or screen vs toy.
- **POV / trending audio** – "POV: you found a toy that keeps them busy for an hour".
- **Mascot Wudi presents** – animated mascot introduces the new toy (I can make these).
- **New arrival / launch** – countdown story + reveal Reel.
- **Customer review** – parent's text review over product shots.
