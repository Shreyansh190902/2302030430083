# Wudly – Reel Scripts WITH Kids + How to Shoot

## Before the shoot (must-do)
- **Written consent** from parents/guardians for every child (model release for social media + ads).
- A **parent/guardian on set at all times**; never leave a child alone with crew.
- **Age-right toys only** (match catalogue age), check for loose small parts with under-3s.
- **Short sessions:** max 20–30 min per child, snack & play breaks, stop if the child is tired or upset.
- **Let them play naturally** – don't force lines; film real reactions. Use toys the child hasn't seen before for genuine excitement.
- Kids in **plain clothes** (white, cream, pastel, or red tee) – no big logos.
- Keep the set **safe**: soft rug, no sharp furniture edges, no cables on the floor, warm room.

## Camera setup
- Phone/camera at the **child's eye level** (sit on the floor).
- Soft window light from the side; white reflector on the other side.
- Shoot 4K 30fps (60fps for slow-motion moments).
- Background: cream wall / Montessori-style room with a plant + Wudly red accents.
- Record **wide (whole scene)** + **close-up (hands & toy)** + **reaction (face)** for every moment.
