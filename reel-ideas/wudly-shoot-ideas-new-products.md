# Wudly – Shoot Ideas: Crack Code, Tool Kit, Barrier Minds, Step Bloom, Easel, Joyboard

Rule: hands / feet / product only – no children's faces. Vertical 9:16 for Reels + a few 4:5 stills for posts.

## 1. Crack Code (Educational logic game · 10+ yrs · Rs. 1,499)
- **"Can you crack it?" challenge** – show the code puzzle, pause 3 sec with a timer, reveal the answer. CTA: "Comment your answer".
- **Spy / detective theme** – dark table, torch light, magnifying glass, text "Mission: Crack the Code 🕵️".
- **Parent vs kid duel** – two pairs of hands, split screen, who solves first.
- **Stills:** overhead flat-lay of all pieces + box; close-up macro of the code pegs.

## 2. My First Tool Kit
- **Little builder POV** – small hands tighten a bolt, hammer a peg; sound on.
- **"Fix it" story** – a broken wooden chair/toy → tool kit comes out → "fixed!" text.
- **Unboxing** – open the box, lay out each tool one by one in a neat knolling flat-lay.
- **Dad & me** – adult hand guides small hand on the spanner (no faces).
- **Stills:** tools laid out on a workbench / cream cloth; tool kit on red Wudly backdrop.

## 3. Barrier Minds (Strategy game · 10+ yrs · Rs. 1,399)
- **Family game night** – 4 hands around the board, warm lamp light, snacks on the side.
- **"How to play in 15 sec"** – text steps over overhead shot.
- **Big move moment** – slow-motion of the winning barrier placement + "Checkmate-level move 😮".
- **Screen-free challenge** – phone placed face down, board comes out.
- **Stills:** board mid-game from a 45° angle; box + pieces flat-lay.

## 4. Step Bloom wooden baby walker (9 months – 1.5 years · Rs. 1,899)
- **First steps (feet only)** – low camera at floor level, only baby feet + walker wheels rolling on wooden floor. Text: "Their first steps 👣".
- **Walker tour** – 360° turn of the walker, close-ups of rounded edges, wheels, activity panel.
- **Assembly in 30 sec** – timelapse of putting it together.
- **Living room lifestyle** – walker in a bright Montessori room with plants and a rug (no child).
- **Gift reveal** – walker with a big red bow: "Best first-birthday gift 🎁".
- **Stills:** side profile on white/cream; walker on red backdrop; detail macro of wood grain.
- *Safety note: if any shot includes a baby, an adult must hold/spot them; show only feet/hands.*

## 5. Tabletop Easel Board
- **Fast-motion drawing** – blank board → finished drawing in 10 sec.
- **Two sides, two ways** – flip from chalkboard to whiteboard (if dual-sided).
- **Daily routine board** – write "Today: Play · Read · Draw" each morning – series.
- **Learning corner setup** – easel on a small table with crayons, chalk and a plant.
- **Stills:** easel 3/4 angle with a cute drawing on it; flat-lay of easel + art supplies.

## 6. Joyboard threading & pattern set (5–7 yrs · Rs. 999)
- **ASMR threading** – lace going through holes, close-up, slow-motion.
- **Copy the pattern** – show a pattern card, then hands copy it on the board; "Can your child do this?"
- **Pattern of the week** – new pattern every week (series).
- **Calm-down activity** – soft light, quiet music: "10 minutes of calm focus".
- **Stills:** finished colourful pattern overhead; laces and board flat-lay.

## Common shoot setup
- **Backgrounds:** Wudly red wall/paper, cream cloth, light wooden table.
- **Light:** soft daylight from a window + one white reflector.
- **Props:** plants, books, small baskets, Wudly box & tissue paper.
- **Shot list per product:** 1 hero still, 1 flat-lay, 2 detail close-ups, 1 Reel (10–20 sec), 1 unboxing clip.
- **After the shoot:** send me the clips/photos and I'll add the logo, text, pills and end card in the ReWamp red style.
