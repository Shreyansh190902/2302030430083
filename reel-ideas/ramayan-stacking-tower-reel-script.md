# Reel Script – Ramayan Stacking Tower: "Har Block Ek Kahaani" 🪔
**Product:** Wudly Ramayan Stacking Tower – wooden stacking blocks with Ramayan questions (English & Hindi) + pictures · "Where Every Block Tells a Story"
**Length:** 30 sec (+ 15 sec cut-down) · 9:16 · **Talent:** Mom + son (or family) · **Language:** Hindi dialogue, English on-screen text
**Post:** Diwali week (5–8 Nov) · **Link:** wudlytoys.com *(add product link once live)*

**Idea:** Diwali evening. Every block they pull has a Ramayan question. Answer right → keep playing. The tower falls on the last question… "Ravan ki haar!" Learning the Ramayan, one block at a time.

---

## Set & props
- Rug, low wooden table, rangoli, marigold garland, **LED diyas** near the child (real diyas only in the background with an adult)
- Ramayan Stacking Tower – tower built, blue box standing beside it, logo facing camera
- Outfits: Mom saree/kurta (red/cream), son mustard/cream kurta
- Camera: eye-level tripod + overhead · 60 fps for block pulls and the fall · macro of a question block

## 15-sec cut-down
Hero shot (2s) → question block macro + "Shri Ram ke pita kaun the?" (4s) → "Raja Dashrath!" (2s) → slow-mo fall + "Ravan ki haar!" (4s) → end card (3s)

## Must-have shots
- Box hero shot (no people) among diyas – for posts too
- Macro of 2–3 question blocks (English & Hindi) – readable on screen
- Son's thinking face and "Sahi jawab!" reaction
- Tower fall from 2 angles (3–4 takes)

## Director tips
- Pick 3 questions the child can answer (Dashrath, Shabri's ber, Ravan) – check they are printed on the blocks and keep those blocks handy.
- Play for real; if the tower falls early, keep it as a blooper for Stories.
- Keep diyas away from where blocks fall; LED diyas only near the child.
- Signed parent consent before filming.
