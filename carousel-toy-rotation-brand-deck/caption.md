Fewer toys out. More play. 🧸🔄
Toy rotation is a simple Montessori trick that brings back focus (and saves your floor!):
1️⃣ Pick 6–8 toys – one to build, one to move, one to think
2️⃣ Swap 2 or 3 every week, on the same day
3️⃣ Store the rest out of sight
4️⃣ Watch what they love and bring it back
