## 1. Where the account is today

| Finding | What it means |
|---|---|
| Avg ~22 likes/post, almost no comments | Posts get seen but give people nothing to respond to. Add a question or call to action to every post. |
| Reels get 600–1,050 plays; the mascot "Meet Wudly" reel got 1,057 | Reels + mascot are the strongest format. Do more. |
| Text-heavy carousel got 12 likes; event announcement got 4 | Long text and pure announcements underperform. Keep text short and make events people-focused. |
| Posts go out at ~10:00 AM IST | Test 8–9 PM IST, when parents are free. |

| Website: placeholder text on banners and gift finder; many best sellers show "Sold out" | Fix before sending traffic. Don't promote sold-out products. |

## 2. Goals for the month

1. **Awareness:** reels average 1,500+ plays (baseline ~700).
2. **Engagement:** 3+ comments per post on average (baseline ~0); saves and shares up.
3. **Traffic and sales:** link clicks to wudlytoys.com, with a Diwali gifting push from 26 Oct.
4. **Credibility:** founder LinkedIn plus ESFE exposure bring in schools, retailers and partners.
5. **Followers:** 1,201 → about 1,500 (stretch). Treat this as a guide, not a promise.

## 4. Formats and cadence

**Instagram: 5 feed posts a week plus daily Stories**

| Day | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Static product spotlight | Sell |
| Tue | Reel | Reach |
| Wed | Parent tip (static or short carousel) | Save and share |
| Thu | Reel (behind the scenes or mascot) | Reach and brand |
| Fri | Offer or social proof | Convert |
| Sat–Sun | Stories only (polls, Q&A, reposts) | Engagement |

**LinkedIn: 2–3 posts a week**, from the founder's profile, with the company page re-sharing.

**Rules for every post:**
- The first line is a hook. Keep captions short (under 8 lines), except for tips.
- End with a single call to action (a question, "save this", or "link in bio").
- Use 5–8 specific hashtags: `#WudlyToys #PlayNaturally #WoodenToys #MontessoriToys #ScreenFreePlay` plus 2–3 topic tags. No keyword lists in brackets.
- Use one phone number and one URL: wudlytoys.com.
- Visual theme: Wudly red `#E31E24` (confirm the exact hex), white, cream `#FBF5EA`, wood tones, rounded font, mascot on hook posts.

## 7. Engagement routine (15 minutes a day)

1. Reply to every comment and DM within a few hours.
2. Post 2–3 Stories daily (polls, quizzes, behind the scenes).
3. Comment on 10 posts by parenting and education accounts.
4. Repost customer content with permission.
5. Each week, reach out to 3–5 parenting micro-influencers with a product-for-review offer.

## 8. Weekly tracking

Track: reel plays, saves, shares, comments per post, profile visits and link clicks. Compare with the baseline above. Keep what beats the average and drop what doesn't.
