# Amdavad Ni Raat: Nine Nights, One Heartbeat
### A Navratri event concept for Ahmedabad

---

## 1. The big idea

Ahmedabad doesn't just *celebrate* Navratri. It is where the whole country comes to see how Navratri is meant to be danced. **Amdavad Ni Raat** builds an event around that pride. For nine nights the venue becomes a living map of the city: the pols, the riverfront, the stepwells, the bazaars and the temples. All of it circles one central **Garbo**, the earthen lamp at the heart of every garba.

> **Positioning line:** *"The world dances garba. Amdavad gives it its heartbeat."*

**Why it works**
- **Heritage credibility:** Ahmedabad is India's first UNESCO World Heritage City, and in 2023 UNESCO added Garba of Gujarat to its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage. The concept uses both facts.
- **Sense of belonging:** every zone is named after a part of the city that Amdavadis know and love.
- **Shareable moments:** each night has its own colour, story and photo moment, which gives people nine reasons to come back.

## 3. Venue layout: "The City in a Circle"

The ground is laid out as a mandala. The goddess sits at the centre, and the city wraps around her.

```
                    [ MAIN STAGE: "Bhadra Mandap" ]
                              |
     [ Pol Ni Gali ]   (  OUTER GARBA RING  )   [ Manek Chowk Food Street ]
                      (   ( INNER RING )   )
                      (   (  THE GARBO  )   )
     [ Vav Lounge ]    (   (  & Aarti   )   )   [ Kala Bazaar Crafts ]
                      (____________________)
                              |
          [ Sabarmati Walk: Entry Promenade & Photo Arches ]
```

| Zone | Inspired by | What happens there |
|---|---|---|
| **Sabarmati Walk** (entry) | Sabarmati Riverfront | A 150 m promenade lined with lanterns, a reflecting-pool strip and lit arches. The first "wow" moment and a ticket-scan funnel |
| **The Garbo** (centre) | The sacred garbi lamp | A giant 6–8 m perforated "earthen" Garbo sculpture with a programmable inner light. Mataji's sthapana sits at its base |
| **Inner & Outer Rings** | Traditional concentric garba | Inner ring for traditional, slower *be taali* and *tran taali* garba. Outer ring for high-energy dodhiyu and dandiya raas |
| **Bhadra Mandap** (stage) | Bhadra Fort & Bhadrakali temple | Main stage for the live orchestra and folk singers. Carved façade in the style of the Pol havelis |
| **Pol Ni Gali** | Old-city pols | A recreated pol lane with wooden jharokhas, chabutro (bird feeder), otla seating and pol-style doorways. Photo zone and heritage storytelling |
| **Vav Lounge** | Adalaj / Rani ki Vav stepwells | Premium seating lounge with stepped tiers and carved-pillar panels. Cool, calm and a little away from the crowd |
| **Manek Chowk Food Street** | Manek Chowk night market | Live Gujarati street food (see §7) |
| **Kala Bazaar** | Law Garden market | Bandhani, mirror-work chaniya cholis, oxidised jewellery, kediyu, artisan stalls |

## 4. Décor and visual identity

**Palette:** marigold saffron, kumkum red, parrot green, peacock blue and gold. Every night the lighting is retuned to that night's Navratri colour.

**Signature décor elements**
1. **Abhla (mirror-work) canopy:** a giant overhead canopy of Kutchi-style mirror-work panels that scatter the stage lights across the dancers.
2. **10,000 Diya Garbi Wall:** a perforated-pot lantern wall around the outer ring. It is lit in a slow wave at the start of each aarti.
3. **Bandhani fabric drapes:** tie-dye drapes on every gateway, in a different pattern for each zone.
4. **Toran gateways:** marigold-and-mango-leaf torans at every entry, made fresh each night.
5. **Kite sky:** a nod to Uttarayan, the city's other great festival. Hundreds of illuminated paper-kite installations hang over the food street.
6. **Pithora and Rogan art murals:** commissioned panels by tribal and Kutchi artisans, crediting the artists by name.
7. **Projection mapping:** each night the Garbo sculpture and stage façade come alive with a 5-minute story of that night's goddess form.

**Sustainability:** clay and natural-fibre décor, reusable LED diyas, refill water stations, no single-use plastic in the food zone, and segregated waste bins with volunteer "Swachh Sena".

## 5. Nine nights, nine stories

Each night honours one form of Navadurga, with a matching theme, colour and special moment. Dress-code colours follow that year's published Navratri colour calendar.

| Night | Goddess | Night theme | Special moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shailputri | **Sthapana Night: "The Beginning"** | Ghatasthapana ceremony; the Garbo is lit for the first time with the 10,000-diya wave |
| 2 | Brahmacharini | **Pol Night** | Heritage-pol walk performances; best traditional attire (heritage look) |
| 3 | Chandraghanta | **Raas Night** | Dandiya raas showcase; mass dandiya attempt |
| 4 | Kushmanda | **Family Night** | Kids' garba circle, grandparents' honour aarti, family-matching-outfit awards |
| 5 | Skandamata | **Folk Roots Night** | Kutchi, Saurashtra and tribal (Rathwa, Dang) folk troupes on stage |
| 6 | Katyayani | **Youth Night** | College garba-team face-off; the most energetic block of the season |
| 7 | Kalaratri | **Shakti Night** | Women-led orchestra and singers; tribute to women changemakers of Gujarat |
| 8 | Mahagauri | **Maha Aarti Night** | Synchronised maha aarti with every attendee holding an LED diya |
| 9 | Siddhidatri | **Grand Finale: "Rang Amdavad"** | Final crowning of the "Garba Samrat & Samragni", drone show and a closing raas until the last beat |
| Dussehra | Victory | **Vijay Utsav** | Daytime fafda-jalebi breakfast festival and a symbolic, eco-friendly Ravan Dahan show |

## 6. Engaging activities

### On the dance floor
- **Live orchestra and folk singers:** traditional garba, then fusion sets, then high-tempo dodhiyu. The **traditional first hour** is protected every night.
- **Garba Gurukul (6:30–7:30 PM):** free beginner workshops on the steps (be taali, tran taali, popatiyu, hinch, dodhiyu). Ideal for first-timers, NRIs and tourists.
- **Garba Samrat & Samragni:** nightly judged prizes for best dancer, best attire, best couple, best group and best kids' performer. Winners go on to the finale.
- **Silent Garba Circle:** an after-hours headphone garba zone, so dancing can continue past amplified-sound limits.

### Culture and experience
- **Pol Storytellers:** short 10-minute performances in Pol Ni Gali about Ahmedabad's history, from Ahmed Shah's founding of the city to the Sabarmati Ashram.
- **Chaniya Choli Atelier:** watch bandhani tying and mirror-work embroidery live, then customise a dupatta.
- **Mehendi and tattoo booths** with Navratri motifs.
- **Heritage photo arches:** a recreated Sidi Saiyyed jali window, a stepwell frame and a giant mirror-work "Garbo" selfie point.

### Digital layer
- **Event app / WhatsApp bot:** passes, nightly schedule, live crowd-density map, lost-and-found, and food-stall pre-ordering.
- **#AmdavadNiRaat reels challenge:** the best garba reel each night appears on the big screen. Weekly winners get finale VIP passes.
- **Nine-Night Passport:** scan a QR at each night's special moment. Collect 7 or more stamps to win a limited-edition brass garbi keepsake.

### For everyone
- **Kids' Chhota Garba ring** with a supervised play area and wristband ID tags.
- **Senior seating** with a close view of the inner ring and a dedicated aarti section.
- **Accessibility:** wheelchair-accessible paths, a ramped viewing deck, sign-language interpretation at aarti on Night 4, and a sensory-friendly quiet zone.

## 9. Safety and operations

- **Crowd management:** separate entry and exit gates, a capacity cap per ring, live CCTV monitoring and a crowd-density dashboard.
- **Women's safety:** the "Shakti Squad" of trained female volunteers and security, a panic-button feature in the app, well-lit parking, and a she-shuttle to metro and BRTS stops.
- **Medical:** two first-aid posts, an ambulance on standby, hydration points every 50 m (dancing for hours is hard work).
- **Mobility:** shuttle tie-ups with Ahmedabad Metro and BRTS, managed parking with QR slots, a ride-share pick-up bay.
- **Compliance:** police, fire, and noise-limit permissions, plus insurance. Sound engineering is tuned to stay within permitted dB limits.
- **Lost & Found / Kids' ID:** wristband ID system and a central help desk.

## 10. Marketing campaign: "Tame Kya Ramo Cho?" ("Where are *you* dancing?")

**Phases**
1. **Teaser (6 weeks out):** the Garbo silhouette reveal with the line "The heartbeat is coming home".
2. **Launch (5 weeks out):** venue walkthrough film, ticket launch, early-bird offer.
3. **Garba Gurukul pre-events (4–1 weeks out):** free pop-up workshops at malls, colleges and corporate offices.
4. **Live season:** nightly reels, a live stream for NRI families ("Dance with Amdavad from anywhere"), daily highlight films.
5. **Post-event:** a recap film, winner features and a "Save the Date" for next year.

**Channels:** Instagram and YouTube reels, local FM radio, hoardings on SG Highway, Ashram Road and CG Road, college ambassadors, and collaborations with local Gujarati creators and singers.

**Partnerships:** ethnic-wear brands (the official chaniya choli partner), jewellers, a local dairy brand (chaas/kulfi), the metro (co-branded shuttles), and Gujarat craft cooperatives.

## 11. Measures of success

- Footfall and season-pass repeat rate (target: 30% or more of attendees come on 3+ nights)
- Social reach of #AmdavadNiRaat and how much user-generated content it produces
- Artisan sales in Kala Bazaar (direct income to craftspeople)
- Safety record: zero major incidents, and how fast help-desk tickets are resolved
- Attendee NPS from the post-event survey

## 12. Signature moment, summarised

> On the final night, the music falls silent. 40,000 people raise LED diyas and the giant Garbo glows from within. A single folk voice begins *"Pankhida o pankhida…"*, the orchestra joins in, and the drones above draw the goddess's trishul over the Ahmedabad sky. Then the whole ground moves together in one last circle.

**Amdavad Ni Raat: Nine nights. One heartbeat. Only in Amdavad.**
