You Can't Prompt a Life.

Inside Sagar Kathrotiya's first TEDx talk

Two years ago, Sagar Kathrotiya started a podcast with no big plan and no idea where it would take him. He just wanted to meet people, have meaningful conversations and learn something new.

It took 57 emails to get the first big yes. Then came more than 100 conversations with founders, artists, doctors and leaders from Gujarat and beyond.

That journey led him to the TEDx stage, with one message: you don't need to know where the road goes. You only need to take the next step.
