# Wudly final theme rules (client approved)

1. Brand red only for CTAs (Swipe, Shop buttons). Headlines in dark brown.
2. 50–60% of every slide is image (top 780 of 1350 px); text below.
3. No extra boxes or shapes. The logo already has its own shape.
4. Full logo in the top-right corner on every slide. Never crop the logo or the bear icon.
5. "Play Naturally" is part of the logo, so it is not repeated anywhere else.
6. Mascot only where it fits naturally (for example, cover and closing slide), not on every slide.
7. No children in photos; toys only.
