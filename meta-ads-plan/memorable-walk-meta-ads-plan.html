02 · Objectives

Recommended campaign objectives

Event decor is visual, personal, and priced per setup. People want to see the work, then talk to someone about their date, space and budget. The objectives below are chosen to get that conversation started.

Objective (in Ads Manager)RoleWhy it fits
Leads → Messaging apps (WhatsApp)PrimaryMatches how bookings already happen (by phone). A tap opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message, so the team can ask about date, venue and budget and share photos straight away.
Leads → Instant formTestCollects event type, date, area and budget before anyone talks. Useful if the team can't reply to chats quickly, or to filter out low-fit inquiries. Use the “Higher intent” form type to reduce junk leads.
Engagement → Video viewsSupport (Growth & Scale budgets only)Cheaply shows setup videos to many local people, then builds a warm audience to retarget with inquiry ads.

We are not recommending Traffic or Reach as main objectives. They bring clicks and views, but Meta will then find people who click, not people who inquire.

05 · Creative

Creative concepts

All concepts use the client's real photos and videos of completed setups. Real work builds more trust than stock images in this category. A6

Carousel

Decor idea lookbook

Taken directly from the reference post. Each card shows one complete setup with a short label (for example, theme or occasion). The last card is a clear “Message us to book your date” slide with the WhatsApp number.

Reel · 15–30 sec

From empty space to celebration

A time-lapse of a room or venue being transformed, ending on the finished setup. The first 2 seconds show the final result so viewers stop scrolling.

Reel or carousel

In the details

Close-ups of the elements that make a setup special, such as backdrops, florals, lighting and table details, depending on what the client uses. Shows quality and care.

Video or image

Client reactions

A family or host seeing the finished setup, or a short quote from a happy client. Only with the client's permission. Best for retargeting.

Carousel or Story

How booking works

Three simple steps: message us with your date and space, choose a theme and budget, we set it up. Removes uncertainty for first-time customers. The steps should be confirmed against the real process.

Reel or carousel

Haldi & wedding functions

A walk-through of a haldi setup, then other wedding function setups the client has done. Shows the range across a wedding, so couples consider one decorator for every function.

Reel · 9:16

Birthday theme reveal

A quick reveal of a themed birthday setup, from the entrance to the cake table, ending on the child's or host's reaction. Rotate several themes so parents see options.

Seasonal

Festival decor ideas

A refreshed lookbook launched ahead of each festival the client serves (for example, the next Ganpati season), with a prompt to book early. Use urgency only if dates or capacity really are limited.

Setting a target cost per booking

There is no past ad data yet, so we will set cost targets from real results after week 2 rather than guess now. To decide what a good result is, we need the client's average booking value and the share of it they're comfortable spending to win a booking.

Formula

Affordable cost per booking = average booking value × % of revenue for marketing.
Affordable cost per inquiry = affordable cost per booking × inquiry-to-booking rate.

08 · Testing

Testing & optimization plan

First week on a new ad account: because the Meta ad account is new, the first week will not deliver consistently good-quality leads. Meta needs real inquiries to learn who to show the ads to. We use this week to gather data, and we judge results from week 2 onward.

Test one thing at a time, so we know what made the difference.

WeeksWhat we testHow we decide
1–2Creative format: carousel vs Reel vs single imageCost per conversation started, and which ad brings qualified chats
3–4Audience: Broad local vs Weddings & haldi vs Birthdays & partiesCost per qualified inquiry
5–6Conversion route: WhatsApp vs Instant formInquiry-to-booking rate, not just lead cost
7–8Message angle: ideas/inspiration vs “check your date” vs how booking works; also which occasion (wedding, haldi, birthday) books bestCost per qualified inquiry
9–12Scale what works, refresh creative, retargetBookings and revenue from ads

Rules we follow

  • Let ads run at least 5–7 days before judging them.
  • Avoid major edits during the first 2 weeks, as they restart learning.
  • Pause an ad that spends twice the target cost per inquiry with no inquiry.
  • Increase budget on winners by about 20% every 3–4 days, not all at once.
  • Refresh creative when frequency passes about 3 or click-through rate falls noticeably.

Lead quality loop

  • Label every inquiry in WhatsApp Business: New → Qualified → Quote sent → Booked / Lost.
  • Share a weekly count of each label so we optimize for bookings, not just messages.
  • Ask “How did you hear about us?” to confirm ad-driven bookings.

10 · Timeline

Launch & management timeline

Week 0
Preparation (5–7 days)

Wedding season is close, so aim to launch within a week. Wedding and haldi creative comes first.

  • Confirm the checklist below and agree on budget.
  • Get access to Meta Business Suite, ad account and payment method; link WhatsApp Business.
  • Collect photos and videos; produce 4–6 creatives in 9:16 and 4:5.
  • Set up the welcome message, quick replies and inquiry labels.
Week 1
Launch

Campaign 1 and retargeting go live. Daily checks on spend, ad approval and incoming chats.

What to expect: the Meta ad account is new, so Meta has no past data on who inquires and books. In the first week, expect fewer leads, and some won't be good quality. This is normal for a new account, and lead quality usually improves from week 2 as Meta learns from real inquiries.

Weeks 1–2
Learning period

Minimal changes. Make sure every inquiry gets a fast reply. First short update to the client at the end of week 2.

Weeks 3–4
First optimization

Pause weak ads, shift budget to winners, start the audience test. First monthly report.

Month 2
Refine and grow

Test WhatsApp vs form and new message angles. Add fresh creative. Raise budget on what produces bookings.

Month 3
Scale and review

Full 90-day review of bookings, cost per booking and lessons learned. Agree the next quarter's plan and the seasonal calendar (for example, starting Ganpati ads about 6 weeks before the festival).

Ongoing
Reporting rhythm

Quick weekly update (spend, inquiries, bookings); monthly report with recommendations.
