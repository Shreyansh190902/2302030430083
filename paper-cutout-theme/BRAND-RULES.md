# Wudly carousel brand rules (client approved)

1. Brand red is used mainly for CTAs (Swipe, Shop buttons). Headings are dark brown; labels are green.
2. Keep it uncluttered: no extra boxes, ribbons or badges. The logo already has its own shape.
3. Logo always in the top-right corner, shown in full, with no box behind it.
4. Never crop the brand icon (bear on stacked pebbles): use it whole or not at all.
5. "Play Naturally" is part of the logo; don't repeat it separately.
6. Mascot only where it fits naturally (e.g. cover, one content slide, CTA), not on every slide.
7. Toys are cut out and placed directly on the scene with a soft shadow.
