# Scene Media – 20 Second Motion Graphic Intro

**Format:** 1920×1080 · 16:9 · 20 seconds
**Style:** cinematic, bold, high-energy. Black background, brand red `#E50000`, white type (matches the logo).
**Files:**
- `index.html` – the animation (open in Chrome)
- `scene-media-intro.webm` – recorded 1080p video
- `logo-icon.png` / `logo-full.png` – your logo, cut out on a transparent background
- `fonts/` – Archivo Black + Poppins, bundled so it works offline

## Voiceover (read-through, about 20 seconds)

> *(0:03)* Create. *(0:04.5)* Capture. *(0:06)* Inspire.
> *(0:13.7)* Scene Media.
> *(0:15.5)* Every story deserves a scene.

**Tone:** deep, confident, cinematic trailer voice. Short pause after each of the first three words so they land on the beats.

## Music suggestion
A cinematic trailer or hybrid-hit track at about 100–120 BPM: a quiet build (0–7s), a big impact at 0:08, and a resolve at 0:15.
Search for "cinematic logo reveal" or "trailer hit intro" in YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music or Epidemic Sound.

## How to use / edit

1. **Preview:** open `scene-media-intro/index.html` in Chrome. Click **↻ Replay** (bottom right) to watch again.
2. **Change words:** edit `CREATE / CAPTURE / INSPIRE` and the tagline near the bottom of `index.html`.
3. **Change colour:** edit `--red` at the top of the `<style>` block.
4. **Export a clean video:** open `index.html?clean` (hides the replay button) full screen and screen-record for 20 s,
   or use the ready-made `scene-media-intro.webm`. Add the music and voiceover in CapCut, Premiere or DaVinci Resolve.
