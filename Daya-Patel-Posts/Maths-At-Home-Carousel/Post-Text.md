"My child is scared of maths."

In 20 years of teaching, I've heard this from parents of toddlers and from JEE aspirants alike.

But here's what I've learned: fear of maths rarely starts with maths. It starts when numbers feel like something on a worksheet instead of something in real life.

The good news? Your home is already a maths classroom.

5 playful ways to build your child's number sense, no worksheets needed:

1️⃣ Count everything: stairs, spoons, buttons. Touch each one as you count.
2️⃣ Sort the laundry: by colour, by size, then into pairs.
3️⃣ Play "more or less" with two bowls of snacks.
4️⃣ Go on a shape hunt: a plate, a tile, a samosa!
5️⃣ Cook together: measure, count, and split a roti into halves and quarters.

None of these need special equipment. Just a few minutes, a curious child and a parent who plays along.

This is exactly how we design every toy at Wudly: start with what a child needs to learn, then make it something they want to touch and play with.

Maths isn't a subject. It's a way of seeing the world.
