About

I’ve spent 20 years helping people learn, from IIM Ahmedabad to a school classroom in Hansol. Today I design the toys that teach.

I’m Daya Patel: mathematician, educator, and Founder & Director of Wudly Toys, where we make wooden educational toys built around real learning goals.

Why toys? In 20 years of teaching, from MBA, BCA and Pharmacy students to JEE aspirants, I saw that people understand fastest when an idea connects to real life. The moment that made me want to build learning toys came while teaching mathematics, computers and English to underprivileged children with Prof. V. L. Mote: when we used physical teaching aids and pretend play, the children enjoyed learning, understood faster and remembered longer. Most toys entertain. Very few are designed backwards from what a child needs to learn. Wudly exists to close that gap.

What makes Wudly different

  • Every toy starts with a learning outcome, mapped to foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN) and NEP 2020 goals
  • Designed by an educator and tested in real classrooms and homes
  • Natural wood, child-safe finishes, made to last
  • A range for every stage, from infant and toddler toys to classroom kits for ages 0 to 10+
  • An Education Series covering English and Hindi language, maths, social and environment, art and life skills

Recognition

Honoured as Innovator in Foundational Literacy and Numeracy Solutions at the Early Years Educators Summit (EYES) 2026, presented by Dr Kiran Bedi.
