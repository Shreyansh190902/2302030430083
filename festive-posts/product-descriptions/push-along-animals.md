**Push them, roll them, make them roar!** 🐶🦒🐘🦁
Four friendly wooden animals on wheels: a playful puppy, a tall giraffe, a gentle elephant and a smiling lion. Each one is brightly painted and rolls smoothly on chunky wooden wheels, ready for little hands to push across the floor.

## How to play
1. Hold an animal by its body and push it along the floor or table.
2. Line all four up for an animal parade.
3. Race them side by side: who reaches the cushion first?
4. Make each animal's sound as it rolls: woof, trumpet, roar!

## What it builds
- 🚶 **Gross motor skills:** crawling and walking after a rolling toy.
- ✋ **Grip and fine motor skills:** chunky shapes that are easy to hold.
- 🐾 **Animal recognition:** names, sounds and features (long neck, big ears, mane).
- 🎨 **Colours:** each animal has its own colour wheels.
- 💡 **Cause and effect:** push it, and it rolls!
- 🌈 **Imagination:** parades, races and jungle stories.

## Play ideas
- **Jungle walk:** take the animals on a trip around the house.
- **Where's the giraffe?** Hide one and let your child find it.
- **Big and tall:** compare the animals by size and height.
- **Story time:** act out a story with all four friends.

## What's in the box
- 4 wooden push-along animals on wheels: dog, giraffe, elephant and lion

Sturdy wooden animals with smooth wooden wheels, no batteries and no noise: simple toys that keep rolling for years.
