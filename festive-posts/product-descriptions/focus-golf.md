**Aim, tap and score through the animal parade!** 🐶🦁🐘
Wudly Focus Golf turns any floor into a mini golf course. Line up 12 friendly wooden animals, take aim with the mallet and gently tap the ball through their arches. It's simple enough for little hands and fun enough for the whole family.

## How to play
1. Stand the animal arches anywhere: in a straight line, a zig-zag, or around the room.
2. Tap the ball with the mallet and guide it through each arch.
3. Count your taps and try to get through all 12 animals in as few taps as possible!
4. Make it harder by spacing the arches further apart or adding turns.

## What it builds
- 🎯 **Focus and concentration:** lining up every shot takes calm attention.
- ✋ **Hand–eye coordination:** controlling the mallet and the ball together.
- 💪 **Gross motor skills and balance:** bending, swinging and moving around the course.
- 🔢 **Counting:** keeping score of taps and arches.
- 🐾 **Animal recognition:** name each animal and its sound as you play.
- 🤝 **Patience and turn-taking:** perfect for playing with siblings, parents and grandparents.

## Play ideas
- **Animal call-out:** say the animal's name or sound before you hit through its arch.
- **Colour route:** go through the animals in colour order.
- **Family tournament:** everyone plays the same course and the fewest taps wins.

## What's in the box
- 1 wooden mallet
- 1 ball
- 12 wooden animal arches with stands: dog, cow, donkey, rhino, lion, bear, sheep, horse, hen, elephant, tiger and zebra

## Why wooden?
Wudly toys are made from wood, so they're sturdy and long-lasting, and they invite active, screen-free play indoors or out.
