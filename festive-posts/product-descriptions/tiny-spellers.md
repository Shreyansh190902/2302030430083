**See it, say it, spell it!** 🦁🐻🚗🥁
Wudly Tiny Spellers are colourful wooden word boards that turn spelling into a picture puzzle. Each board shows a friendly picture (like a lion, a bear, a car or a drum) next to letter-shaped slots. Children find the right wooden letters and fit them in to spell the word.

## How to play
1. Pick a board and name the picture together: "Lion!"
2. Sound out the word slowly: l-i-o-n.
3. Find each wooden letter and fit it into its letter-shaped slot.
4. Read the finished word out loud, then mix the letters and try another board!

## Word types included
- **Consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC) words:** the first simple words children learn to read.
- **4-letter blend words:** two consonant sounds blended together.
- **Digraph words:** two letters that make one sound.

## What it builds
- 📖 **Early spelling and reading:** picture, sound and letters together.
- 👂 **Phonics:** hearing each sound in a word.
- 🔤 **Letter recognition:** matching letter shapes to slots.
- 💬 **Vocabulary:** new words with pictures to remember them.
- ✋ **Fine motor skills:** picking up and fitting letter pieces.
- 🌟 **Confidence:** "I spelled it myself!"

## Play ideas
- **Letter hunt:** call out a letter sound and find it in the pile.
- **Picture first:** cover the word, show the picture and guess the spelling.

## What's in the box
- Wooden picture word boards with letter-shaped slots
- Colourful wooden letter pieces

## A perfect gift
A screen-free learning gift for little learners getting ready for reading and writing.

## Why wooden?
Sturdy wooden boards and letters that little hands can hold, fit and play with again and again, with no screens and no batteries.
