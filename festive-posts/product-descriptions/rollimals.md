**Push, roll and zoom – little wheels, big adventures!** 🦆🐘🚌
Wudly Rollimals is a family of 10 chunky wooden animals and vehicles that roll smoothly on big wooden wheels. Easy for tiny hands to grip and push, they turn the floor into a road, a farm and a whole world of pretend play.

## How to play
1. Grip a Rollimal by its rounded body and push it along the floor or table.
2. Build a road with books or cushions and send the vehicles on a trip.
3. Line them up for a parade, or race two side by side.
4. Make the sounds together: quack for the duck, toot for the elephant, beep-beep for the cars!

## What it builds
- ✋ **Grip and fine motor skills:** chunky shapes that are easy to hold.
- 🚶 **Gross motor skills:** crawling and walking after a rolling toy.
- 💡 **Cause and effect:** push it, and it rolls!
- 🗣️ **Language and sounds:** naming animals and vehicles and making their sounds.
- 🌈 **Imagination:** roads, farms and stories made up by your child.
- 🧩 **Sorting:** grouping animals and vehicles.

## Play ideas
- **Animal parade:** line them up and name each one as it rolls past.
- **Bus stop:** the bus picks up the animals and drops them home.
- **Roll and find:** roll one away and ask your little one to bring it back.
- **Storytime:** act out a story with the whole Rollimals family.

## What's in the box
- 10 wooden Rollimals with wooden wheels, including a duck, an elephant, a bus, a truck and cars

## Why wooden?
Smooth, natural-wood finish with no batteries and no noise: just simple, sturdy toys that roll for years and get passed on from sibling to sibling.
