**Scoop up colours, count to 10 – it's ice cream time!** 🍦🌈
Wudly Magnetic Maze Ice Cream is a wooden maze board full of colourful beads and 10 ice-cream cones. Children use the magnetic pen to guide each bead along the maze into the matching cone, learning colours and numbers 1 to 10 with every scoop.

## How to play
1. Place the magnetic pen on the board over a bead to "pick it up".
2. Slide it slowly through the maze paths.
3. Drop it into the cone with the same colour.
4. Fill each cone with the right number of scoops: 1 blue, 2 red, 3 yellow… all the way to 10 black!

## Colours and numbers
1 Blue · 2 Red · 3 Yellow · 4 Green · 5 Brown · 6 Orange · 7 Purple · 8 Pink · 9 White · 10 Black

## What it builds
- 🎨 **Colour recognition:** 10 colours, each with its own cone.
- 🔢 **Counting 1–10:** every cone shows how many scoops it needs.
- ✏️ **Fine motor skills and pencil grip:** steering the magnetic pen.
- 👀 **Hand–eye coordination:** guiding beads through the maze.
- 🧘 **Focus and patience:** slow, careful moves win.
- 🧩 **Problem solving:** finding the right path to each cone.

## Play ideas
- **Ice cream shop:** "One strawberry scoop, please!" Your child serves the order.
- **Beat the timer:** how fast can you fill one cone?
- **Count out loud:** say each number as the scoops go in.
