# Owner-profile festival greetings – cream wave version

Background: the cream grid design with red waves that you supplied. The greeting is in maroon Hindi with the red Wudly logo.


Each festival comes in two sizes:
- **Post:** 1080×1350 (`*-post.png`)
- **Story:** 1080×1920 (`*-story.png`)

| Festival | Hindi | Wish line |
|---|---|---|
| Dhanteras | शुभ धनतेरस | May this Dhanteras bring good health, wealth and happiness to your home. |
| Diwali | शुभ दीपावली | May the festival of lights fill your home with joy, love and togetherness. |
| New Year | नूतन वर्षाभिनंदन | Wishing you a new year full of fresh beginnings and happy moments. |
| Bhai Dooj | भाई दूज की शुभकामनाएँ | Celebrating the beautiful bond between brothers and sisters. |
