# Wudly Dussehra Post – Concept Note

## Concept name
**"Ten Heads. One Little Hero."**

## The big idea
Dussehra is the story of good winning over evil. Instead of a regular greeting with Ravan, diyas or fireworks, we tell it the Wudly way, through play.

Our Ravan isn't scary. He's built from **colourful painted wooden toy blocks**, like something a child would stack in their play corner. His ten heads are ten little wooden cubes, each with a crown and a cute frown. Facing him is the **Wudly mascot, our "little hero"**, who has just let go of a **wooden arrow** that's flying with a trail of gold sparkles.

The message is that **every block that falls is a small win for goodness.** Dussehra becomes a play moment, and the post is a festive greeting and a quiet showcase of our wooden-block craft in one.

## Why it works
- **Different from every other Dussehra post.** Most brands use the same Ravan, diya and bow-arrow clip-art. A toy-block Ravan stands out in the feed and makes people stop and look.
- **On-brand.** It shows what Wudly makes (wooden blocks, natural colours, safe and playful) without a product-ad feel.
- **Child-friendly.** Ravan is cute, not frightening, so parents can share it happily.
- **The mascot has a role.** The mascot isn't just decoration; it's the hero of the story.
- **Easy to read.** One headline, one visual story, one closing line.

## Visual elements
| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Toy-block Ravan (10 heads) | Hero visual, made from colourful painted wooden blocks |
| Wudly mascot | The "little hero" who shoots the arrow (bottom-left) |
| Wooden arrow + gold sparkle trail | Action and movement, the moment goodness wins |
| Warm orange glow behind Ravan | Dussehra fire/festive feel without showing real flames |
| Red Studio background | Brand red, a premium studio look |
| Reversed (cream) Wudly logo | Top-right, as per brand rules |

## Copy
- **Small tag:** HAPPY DUSSEHRA
- **Headline:** Ten heads. *One little hero.*
- **Closing line:** May goodness win, in play and in life. Happy Dussehra from Wudly
- **Footer:** @wudly_toys · wudlytoys.com

## Brand rules followed
- Logo top-right, full and uncut
- Brand red used as the main colour; no clutter, boxes or extra shapes
- Mascot used where it fits naturally (as the hero)
- "Play Naturally" not repeated, since it's already in the logo
- No children in the creative

## Caption hook
> Ten heads. One little hero. 🏹✨
> This Dussehra, our little hero is taking on a Ravan built from wooden toy blocks, because every block that falls is a small win for goodness.
