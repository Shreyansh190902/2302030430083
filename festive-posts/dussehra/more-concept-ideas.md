# Wudly Dussehra – More Creative Concept Ideas

Each idea is built around wooden toys, has no kids, and follows the brand rules (logo top-right, brand red mainly for CTAs, less clutter, mascot only where it fits).

---

## 1. "The Tower Falls" (stacking-tower Ravan)
**Visual:** A tall wooden stacking tower shaped like Ravan with ten tiny heads on top. One wooden ball rolls in and the tower is mid-tumble, with blocks flying in the air.
**Headline:** *Some towers are meant to fall.*
**Line:** This Dussehra, let every bad habit tumble down.
**Format:** Single post, or a stop-motion reel with real Wudly blocks.

## 2. "10 Heads, 10 Bad Habits" (carousel)
**Visual:** Each slide shows one wooden block head labelled with a bad habit, and an arrow knocks it off.
- Slide 1: *Ravan had 10 heads. So do our bad habits.*
- Slides 2–9: Too much screen time · Junk food · Sleeping late · Not sharing · Shouting · Breaking toys · Wasting food · Plastic toys 😉
- Slide 10: *Knock them down, one block at a time. Happy Dussehra!*
**Why:** Relatable for parents, saveable and shareable, with a gentle product nudge on the "plastic toys" head.

## 3. "Good vs Evil, Toy Edition"
**Visual:** A split layout. On the left, a messy pile of broken plastic toys in dull grey tones (evil). On the right, neat, warm Wudly wooden toys in sunlight (good).
**Headline:** *Choose the good side.*
**Line:** Safe, natural, made to last, just like goodness.
**Why:** A strong brand-message post that ties Dussehra to the "why wooden" story.

## 4. "Mini Ram Leela Theatre"
**Visual:** A small wooden puppet theatre stage with a red curtain. Wooden peg dolls play Ram, Lakshman, Hanuman and Ravan.
**Headline:** *The greatest story, played the wooden way.*
**Line:** Every Dussehra, the good story gets told again.
**Reel version:** Peg dolls "act out" the story in 15 seconds with a dhol beat.

## 5. "Bow & Arrow Shape Sorter"
**Visual:** A flat-lay of wooden shapes (triangles, arches, sticks) arranged into a bow and arrow on a cream background, Montessori style.
**Headline:** *Aim for good.*
**Line:** Small hands, big lessons, Happy Vijayadashami.
**Why:** Minimal and premium, it fits the client's "less cluttered" rule best.

## 6. "Ravan's Ten Heads = Ten Skills"
**Visual:** Ten wooden cubes in a row, each painted with an icon for a skill that wooden toys build: focus, patience, creativity, balance, colours, counting, motor skills, imagination, problem-solving, confidence.
**Headline:** *Ravan had ten heads. Your child can have ten superpowers.*
**Line:** Let the play win.
**Why:** Turns a villain symbol into a positive learning message for parents.

## 7. "The Last Block" (emotional/minimal)
**Visual:** A dark background with one single wooden block glowing warm orange like a diya flame. Wood shavings fall around it like sparks.
**Headline:** *One small light defeats all darkness.*
**Line:** Happy Dussehra from Wudly.
**Why:** Calm, premium and very different from noisy festival posts.

## 8. "Dussehra Gift Box" (sales angle)
**Visual:** A wooden toy gift set opened up, with the toys arranged like a festive mandala and a small wooden arrow on top.
**Headline:** *Gift something good this Dussehra.*
**CTA (brand red pill):** Shop now · wudlytoys.com
**Use:** A boosted ad or story, optionally with an offer code (placeholder: **DUSSEHRA10**, to be confirmed by the client).

## 9. "Burn the Plastic, Not the Planet"
**Visual:** A Ravan effigy made of plastic toys, with a Wudly wooden arrow aimed at it. No real fire is shown, just a glowing outline.
**Headline:** *This Dussehra, let plastic go.*
**Line:** Choose toys that grow with your child, and with the earth.
**Why:** A strong eco message. Needs a client check first, because it's a bold, slightly negative tone.

## 10. Interactive story series (3 frames)
- Story 1: Poll – *Which bad habit should fall this Dussehra?* 📱 Screen time / 🍟 Junk food
- Story 2: Quiz – *How many heads did Ravan have?* 8 / 10 / 12
- Story 3: The toy-block Ravan post + "Shop wooden toys" link sticker
**Why:** Boosts engagement and reach the day before and on Dussehra.
