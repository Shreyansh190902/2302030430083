# Wudly carousels – Photoshop files

There are 28 layered PSDs, 1080×1350 each, across 4 carousels. They open in Photoshop or free at https://www.photopea.com.

## Layers in every slide (bottom → top)
- **Background:** white (#FCFCFA) or butter yellow (#F9EC9A).
- **Wudly logo**
- **Kicker:** the small dark label, e.g. "FOR LITTLE ARTISTS".
- **Headline:** red, Fuzzy Bubbles mixed with Montserrat.
- **Body text**
- **Product - <name>:** one layer per toy, including its soft shadow.
- **Label - <name>:** the toy name under the product.
- **Footer:** "Swipe →" and the page number.
- **Last slide only:** "Call to action" and "Mascot" layers.

## Changing text
Text is saved as image layers, not live text. To change a line:
1. Hide its layer.
2. Type the new text with the fonts in `fonts/`. Install them first.

| Text | Font | Size (px) | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline (normal words) | Montserrat Medium | ~96 | #E21A1A |
| Headline (handwritten words) | Fuzzy Bubbles Bold | ~100 | #E21A1A |
| Kicker | Montserrat Bold | 28 | #28201C |
| Body text | Montserrat Medium | 32–34 | #28201C (red on covers and last slide) |
| Product labels | Montserrat Bold | 24–25 | #28201C |
| Swipe / page number | Montserrat SemiBold | 24–26 | #E21A1A / #786459 |
| Call to action | Montserrat Bold | 32 | #E21A1A |

Margins: text starts 66 px from the left edge. The logo sits in the top-right corner.
