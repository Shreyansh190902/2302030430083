# Wudly × Diwali – Step Bloom "Pehla Kadam" Reel Script
**Shoot:** 31 October · **Talent:** Mother + son · **Length:** 20 sec · 9:16
**Product:** Wudly Wooden Step Bloom For Kids · 9 months – 1.5 years · Rs. 1,899 (MRP Rs. 2,999)
**Link:** https://www.wudlytoys.com/products/wudly-wooden-step-bloom-for-kids

**Idea:** Big-brother story. The son wraps a Diwali gift for his baby cousin/sibling – it's the Step Bloom. The baby holds the handle and takes a few wobbly steps towards Mom while the son cheers. "Is Diwali, pehla kadam."

*Age note: Step Bloom is for 9 months – 1.5 years, so this reel needs a baby (cousin or sibling, with parent consent). No baby available? Shoot Scenes 1–2 and the end card only (son wraps and gifts it), and use product-only shots for the walking part.*

---

## Set & props
- Step Bloom, wrapping paper + ribbon, marigolds, LED diyas on a shelf (not on the floor), soft rug, clear floor space of ~3 m
- Outfits: Mom red/cream saree or kurta, son mustard/cream kurta, baby in a soft cotton kurta
- Camera: tripod eye-level + overhead arm · 60 fps for slow-mo close-ups

---

## Screenplay

**SCENE 1 – THE SECRET GIFT**
*(Son on the rug, wrapping a big box with Mom's help. Tape sound, ribbon tied.)*
**Mom:** *(whispers)* "Ye kiske liye hai?"
**Son:** *(proud)* "Chhotu ke liye! Uska pehla Diwali gift."
📱 Text: **A big brother's Diwali gift 🎁**

**SCENE 2 – UNWRAP**
*(Slow-mo: son tears the paper, Step Bloom appears. Macro on wood grain, red handle and wheels.)*
**Son:** *(excited)* "Dekho chhotu, tumhari gaadi!"
📱 Text: **Wooden. Sturdy. Made for first steps.**

**SCENE 3 – FIRST STEPS (CHILD EYE-LEVEL, 60 FPS)**
*(Baby holds the handle (Mom/parent right behind). Step Bloom rolls slowly forward, one wobbly step at a time.)*
**Son:** *(clapping, kneeling at the other end)* "Chal chhotu… aaja… aaja!"
📱 Text: **Step… by… step 👣**

**SCENE 4 – THE CHEER**
*(Baby reaches the son; son hugs the baby, Mom claps. Fairy lights and diyas glow behind.)*
**Mom (voice-over):** "Is Diwali… pehla kadam."
📱 Text: **Their first steps, this Diwali 🪔**

**END CARD (3 sec)**
*(Red Wudly card · Step Bloom product shot · logo top-right.)*
📱 Text: **Step Bloom · 9 months – 1.5 yrs · Rs. 1,899 · wudlytoys.com**

## 10-sec cut-down
unwrap (2s) → baby's steps (4s) → hug (2s) → end card (2s)

## Director tips
- Only on a flat floor or rug – no stairs, no diyas on the floor anywhere near the walking path.
- An adult stays right behind the baby every second; the son cheers from the front.
- Shoot the baby part when the baby is fresh (after a nap), short takes only – never force it.
- If the baby doesn't walk on cue, a few pushes with a parent holding still looks great.
- Signed parent consent for both children before filming.
