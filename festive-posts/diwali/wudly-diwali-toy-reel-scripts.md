# Wudly × Diwali – Toy-led Reel Scripts (Mother & Son, 31 Oct)
Each reel = **one hero toy** in a Diwali setting. The toy is always in focus: clear close-ups, logo/label visible, product name + link on the end card.
**Format:** 9:16 · 15–25 sec · Hindi dialogue (natural) · English on-screen text · end card: red Wudly card with product name & wudlytoys.com
**Set:** rug, low wooden table, marigolds, LED diyas near the child (real diyas only in background, handled by an adult), fairy-light bokeh.

**Every reel must have these 3 toy shots:**
1. **Hero shot** – toy alone on the rug among marigolds/diyas (2 sec)
2. **Hands-on close-up** – son's hands using the toy (slow-mo if possible)
3. **Detail macro** – wood grain, colours, the Wudly logo on the toy
