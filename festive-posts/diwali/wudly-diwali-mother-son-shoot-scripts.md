# Wudly × Diwali – Mother & Son Shoot Scripts
**Shoot date:** 31 October · **Talent:** Mother + son (approx. 4–7 yrs – adjust toys/lines to his age)
**Theme:** *"Light up their Diwali the natural way" 🪔* – wooden toys, family time, gifts that last.
**Format:** Instagram Reels, 9:16 vertical, 15–30 sec. Dialogue in Hindi (natural, not memorised – let them speak in their own words). On-screen text in English.

---

## Before the shoot
- **Consent:** signed parent consent (social media + ads) before filming.
- **Outfits:** traditional & coordinated – mom in a red/cream saree or kurta, son in a cream/mustard kurta. Avoid big logos.
- **Set:** living room floor with a rug, low wooden table, marigold garland, 8–10 diyas, fairy lights in the background (soft bokeh), a few cushions.
- **Diya safety:** use **LED diyas** near the child. Real diyas only in the background, lit and handled by an adult, never within the child's reach.
- **Toys needed:** Wudly gift box + tissue paper, Joyboard, Alpho Spell, Spin Alpha, Weather Sort, Recycle Ride, My Farm House, 8 Dice Shut The Box, Memory Chess, a few wooden blocks/beads for rangoli.
- **Camera:** phone 4K 30fps (60fps for slow-mo), tripod + one overhead arm, white reflector. Shoot at the child's eye level.
- **Child care:** short takes (20–30 min blocks), snack breaks, let him play naturally between takes and keep rolling – the best moments are unplanned.
