# Wudly × Diwali – "Ram Jenga" Reel Script (Mother & Son)
**Shoot:** 31 October · **Talent:** Mother + son · **Length:** 30 sec (+ 15 sec cut-down) · 9:16
**Product:** Wudly Wooden Classic Tumbling Tower Game (54 pcs) · 5–7 yrs · Rs. 499
**Link:** https://www.wudlytoys.com/products/wudly®-wooden-tumbling-tower-game-54-pcs-classic-block-stacking-game-for-kids-adults-educational-indoor-party-family-fun-game-handcrafted
*(Alternative: Colorful Stacking Tower, Rs. 499 – if you want coloured blocks for the "Ayodhya" look.)*

**Idea:** Diwali celebrates Shri Ram's return to Ayodhya. Mom and son build **"Ayodhya" with the tumbling tower blocks**, light diyas around it to welcome Ram, then play Jenga on it. Every block they pull, Mom says one small line of the Ramayan story. The tower falls at the end = **"Buraai ki haar, achhai ki jeet"**, and everyone laughs.

---

## Set & props
- Low wooden table on a rug, marigold garland, fairy-light bokeh behind
- **LED diyas** around the tower (real diyas only in the background, handled by mom)
- Small paper flag / bow-arrow cut-out (optional), a few marigold petals
- Outfits: Mom – saree/kurta in red or cream · Son – mustard/cream kurta
- Camera: eye-level on tripod + overhead shot; 60 fps for the tower-fall moment

## 15-sec cut-down (for stories / ads)
1. Blocks out (1s) → 2. Timelapse build (3s) → 3. Diyas placed (2s) → 4. Two careful pulls (4s) → 5. Slow-mo fall (2s) → 6. "Happy Diwali!" + end card (3s)

## Must-have shots
- Hero shot of the tower alone among diyas (before play)
- Macro of wood grain + Wudly logo on the box
- Son's fingers pulling a block (slow-mo)
- The tower falling – shoot 3–4 takes from two angles, keep the best
- Son's laughing reaction after the fall
- Mom & son hug with fairy lights behind

## Director tips
- Let the son say the lines in his own words – keep "Ram ji Ayodhya wapas aaye" as the key line.
- Don't fake the fall: play for real and keep the camera rolling. If it falls early, that's a great blooper for stories.
- Blocks can fall onto the table/rug only – keep diyas a little away from the tower so nothing knocks into them.
- Shoot this **after** the gift-reveal reel (so the son is warmed up), before the break.
