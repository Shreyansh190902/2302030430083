# Wudly × Diwali – Joyboard "Diya Pattern" Reel Script
**Shoot:** 31 October · **Talent:** Mother + son · **Length:** 20 sec (+ 10 sec cut) · 9:16
**Product:** Wudly Wooden Joyboard Educational Toy · 5–7 yrs · Rs. 999
**Link:** https://www.wudlytoys.com/products/wudly-wooden-joyboard-educational-toy-for-kids

**Idea:** Mom shows a simple diya drawing. Son threads the same diya shape on the Joyboard with orange & yellow laces. Calm, satisfying ASMR threading → proud reveal → the "Joyboard diya" glows among real diyas.

---

## Set & props
- Low wooden table, cream cloth, marigolds, LED diyas, fairy-light bokeh behind
- Joyboard + orange, yellow and red laces (pre-sorted)
- A paper card with a simple diya drawing (flame + bowl), drawn in thick marker
- Outfits: Mom – red/cream saree or kurta · Son – mustard/cream kurta
- Camera: tripod eye-level + overhead arm · 60 fps for threading close-ups · mic close for lace sounds

---

## Screenplay

**SCENE 1 – Table, evening (fairy lights on)**
*(Hero shot: Joyboard on the cream cloth between marigolds and two LED diyas. Laces lie beside it.)*
📱 Text: **Can you make a diya? 🪔**

*(Mom sits down next to her son, holding the paper card.)*
**Mom:** *(showing the card)* "Ye bana sakte ho?"
**Son:** *(looks, nods confidently)* "Haan!"

**SCENE 2 – Threading (ASMR, no music, 60 fps)**
*(Overhead close-up. Son's small hands push the orange lace through the first hole and pull it slowly. Soft "shhk" of the lace.)*
📱 Text: **Thread · Pull · Repeat**

*(Cut to side close-up: son concentrating, tongue slightly out. Mom quietly hands him the yellow lace for the flame.)*
**Mom:** *(whisper)* "Ab lau banao…"

*(Overhead: the diya bowl in orange, flame in yellow slowly appears on the board.)*
📱 Text: **10 minutes of calm focus 🧠**

**SCENE 3 – Reveal**
*(Son lifts the Joyboard and holds it next to the paper card – they match.)*
**Son:** *(excited)* "Dekho Mumma! Mera diya!"
**Mom:** *(claps, smiles)* "Wah! Sabse sundar diya!"
📱 Text: **Perfect! ✨**

**SCENE 4 – Glow moment**
*(Son places the Joyboard among the real/LED diyas on the table. Camera slowly pushes in; fairy lights twinkle behind.)*
**Mom (VO):** "Is Diwali, diya bhi khud banaya."
📱 Text: **Made by little hands, this Diwali 🪔**

**END CARD (3 sec)**
*(Red Wudly card · Joyboard product shot · logo top-right.)*
📱 Text: **Joyboard · 5–7 yrs · Rs. 999 · wudlytoys.com**

"Ye bana sakte ho?" (2s) → threading close-ups (4s) → "Dekho Mumma!" (2s) → end card (2s)

## Director tips
- **Pre-practise the diya shape** once off-camera so the son knows the hole pattern – then film the real attempt.
- Keep the diya very simple: 1 bowl (5–6 holes wide) + 1 small flame.
- Film threading for 2–3 minutes continuously; pick the best 8 seconds in edit.
- Record clean lace sound (fan/AC off) – no music in the threading part.
- Keep LED diyas only near the child; real diyas stay in the background with Mom.
