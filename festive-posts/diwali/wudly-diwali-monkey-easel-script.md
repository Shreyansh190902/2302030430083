# Wudly × Diwali – Monkey Easel "Two-Side Rangoli" Reel Script
**Shoot:** 31 October · **Talent:** Mother + son · **Length:** 20 sec · 9:16
**Product:** Wudly Wooden 2-in-1 Whiteboard & Blackboard Monkey Easel Board · 3+ years · Rs. 1,399 (MRP Rs. 2,799)
**Link:** https://www.wudlytoys.com/products/wudly-wooden-2-in-1-whiteboard-blackboard-monkey-easel-board

**Idea:** A rangoli drawing contest on the 2-in-1 easel. Son draws a rangoli on the whiteboard side with markers, Mom draws one on the blackboard side with chalk. The easel turns round for the reveal – Mom declares her son the winner. No colours on the floor, no mess.

---

## Set & props
- Monkey Easel, whiteboard markers (red, orange, yellow, green), coloured chalk, duster, marigolds, LED diyas, fairy lights
- Outfits: Mom red/cream saree or kurta, son mustard/cream kurta
- Camera: tripod eye-level + overhead arm · 60 fps for slow-mo close-ups

---

## Screenplay

**SCENE 1 – THE CHALLENGE**
*(Hero shot: Monkey Easel standing on the rug between marigolds and LED diyas, monkey face smiling at camera.)*
**Son:** "Mumma, rangoli competition?"
**Mom:** *(picks up chalk)* "Done! Tum whiteboard, main blackboard."
📱 Text: **Diwali rangoli… on a board? 🤔**

**SCENE 2 – DRAWING (QUICK CUTS, BOTH SIDES)**
*(Son's side: close-up of the marker drawing circles and petals – squeaky marker sound.)*
*(Mom's side: chalk sketching a diya in the centre – soft scratch sound.)*
📱 Text: **Whiteboard 🖍️ vs Blackboard ✏️**
**Son:** *(peeking round the easel)* "Mumma, copy mat karna!"
**Mom:** *(laughs)* "Tum bhi mat dekho!"

**SCENE 3 – THE REVEAL**
*(Overhead/360 move: the easel is turned slowly – Mom's chalk rangoli, then son's colourful marker rangoli.)*
**Mom:** *(hand on heart)* "Ye wala jeet gaya!"
**Son:** *(jumps)* "Yesss!"
📱 Text: **2 sides. 2 artists. 1 Diwali ✨**

**SCENE 4 – GLOW MOMENT**
*(Son places two LED diyas in front of the easel. Camera pushes in on his rangoli and the monkey face.)*
**Mom (voice-over):** "Is Diwali, rangoli bina rang ke daag ke."
📱 Text: **No mess. Just colour & imagination 🪔**

**END CARD (3 sec)**
*(Red Wudly card · Monkey Easel product shot · logo top-right.)*
📱 Text: **Monkey Easel · 2-in-1 board · 3+ yrs · Rs. 1,399 · wudlytoys.com**

## Director tips
- Lightly pencil-sketch a simple rangoli circle on the son's side beforehand if he needs a guide.
- Keep Mom's chalk rangoli simple (a diya in a circle) so the son's colourful one stands out.
- Shoot a clean product shot of both sides before drawing (for posts and carousels).
- Record marker squeak and chalk scratch sounds clean – great ASMR for the edit.
- Wipe the boards fully between takes; keep a duster and a dry cloth ready.
- Get signed parent consent before filming.
