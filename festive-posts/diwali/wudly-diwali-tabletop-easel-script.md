# Wudly × Diwali – Tabletop Easel "Diwali Card for Mumma" Reel Script
**Shoot:** 31 October · **Talent:** Mother + son · **Length:** 20 sec · 9:16
**Product:** Wudly Wooden 2-in-1 Whiteboard & Blackboard Tabletop Easel Board · 3+ years · Rs. 999 (MRP Rs. 1,899)
**Link:** https://www.wudlytoys.com/products/wudly-wooden-2-in-1-whiteboard-blackboard-tabletop-easel-board

**Idea:** The son is secretly making something on the tabletop easel and won't let Mom look. He turns it round: a drawn diya and "Happy Diwali Mumma ❤️". Mom flips the board to the blackboard side and writes back "Love you beta". The sweetest Diwali card – and it wipes clean for next year.

*Age note: If the son can't write yet, he draws the diya and a heart, and Mom writes the words with him (his hand on hers).*

---

## Set & props
- Tabletop Easel, red/orange/yellow markers, white chalk, duster, marigolds, LED diyas, a cup of chai on the table for Mom
- Outfits: Mom red/cream saree or kurta, son mustard/cream kurta
- Camera: tripod eye-level + overhead arm · 60 fps for slow-mo close-ups

---

## Screenplay

**SCENE 1 – THE SECRET**
*(Son at the dining table, hiding behind the tabletop easel, drawing fast. Mom walks in with her chai.)*
**Mom:** *(curious)* "Kya chhupa rahe ho?"
**Son:** *(arms spread over the board)* "Mat dekho! Surprise hai!"
📱 Text: **He's hiding something… 👀**

**SCENE 2 – MAKING IT (CLOSE-UPS)**
*(Marker close-up: a diya in orange, a yellow flame, a red heart. Little hand, squeaky marker sound.)*
*(Mom pretends to read the newspaper, peeks once; son catches her.)*
**Son:** "Mumma! No peeking!"
📱 Text: **Work in progress 🖍️**

**SCENE 3 – THE REVEAL**
*(Son slowly turns the easel round: diya drawing + "Happy Diwali Mumma ❤️".)*
**Son:** *(shy smile)* "Happy Diwali Mumma!"
**Mom:** *(emotional, hugs him)* "Ye mera sabse pyaara card hai."
📱 Text: **The sweetest Diwali card 💛**

**SCENE 4 – HER REPLY**
*(Mom flips the board to the blackboard side and writes in chalk: "Love you beta 🪔". Son giggles.)*
**Mom (voice-over):** "Kuch cards khareede nahi jaate… banaye jaate hain."
📱 Text: **Made with love, this Diwali 🪔**

**END CARD (3 sec)**
*(Red Wudly card · Tabletop Easel product shot · logo top-right.)*
📱 Text: **Tabletop Easel · 2-in-1 board · 3+ yrs · Rs. 999 · wudlytoys.com**

## 10-sec cut-down
"Mat dekho!" (2s) → marker close-up (2s) → reveal + hug (4s) → end card (2s)

## Director tips
- Do the drawing for real and roll the whole time – then re-shoot the reveal only, once the card is ready.
- Keep the hug real: don't over-direct Mom, let her react to his card.
- Use bold markers so the words read clearly on a phone screen.
- Shoot a clean product shot of both sides of the easel before drawing.
- Get signed parent consent before filming.
