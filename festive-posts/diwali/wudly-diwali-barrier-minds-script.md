# Wudly × Diwali – Barrier Minds "Mumma vs Me" Reel Script
**Shoot:** 31 October · **Talent:** Mother + son · **Length:** 25 sec (+ 12 sec cut-down) · 9:16
**Product:** Wudly Wooden Barrier Minds Strategy Board Game · 10+ yrs · Rs. 1,399 (MRP Rs. 1,799)
**Link:** https://www.wudlytoys.com/products/wudly-wooden-barrier-minds-strategy-board-game

**Idea:** Diwali game night, phones down. Each player has to get their pawn to the other side of the board – and can drop wooden walls to block the other. Mom blocks her son ("Blocked!"), he thinks hard, finds a way around, blocks her back and wins. Hook: **"Plan. Block. Outsmart."**

*Age note: the game is rated 10+. If the son is younger, keep it as Mom teaching him – she explains the rule in one line and "lets" him find the way round. Don't claim it is for younger kids in text or caption.*

---

## Set & props
- Low wooden table, cream cloth, marigolds, LED diyas at the corners of the board, fairy-light bokeh behind
- Barrier Minds board, 2 pawns (red & green), all wall pieces in two neat piles
- Two phones + a small basket or brass plate (for "phones down")
- Outfits: Mom – red/cream saree or kurta · Son – mustard/cream kurta
- Camera: tripod eye-level + overhead arm · 60 fps for wall-drop close-ups · mic close for the wooden "tock"

---

## Screenplay

**SCENE 1 – Phones down (evening, fairy lights on)**
*(Close-up: Mom and son each drop their phone into the basket next to a diya.)*
**Mom:** "Aaj Diwali game night… sirf hum dono."
📱 Text: **Phones down. Game on. 📵🪔**

**SCENE 2 – The challenge**
*(Overhead hero shot: Barrier Minds board among marigolds, pawns on opposite sides, wall piles ready.)*
**Son:** *(rubbing hands)* "Mumma, aaj main jeetunga!"
**Mom:** *(smiles)* "Pehle us paar toh pahuncho!"
📱 Text: **Reach the other side. Block your opponent. 🧠**

**SCENE 3 – Blocked! (60 fps close-up)**
*(Son moves his pawn forward two squares. Mom slowly slides a wall into the groove right in front of him – "tock".)*
**Mom:** "Blocked!" 😏
**Son:** *(hands on head)* "Nooo!"
📱 Text: **Mom said: not today 😂**

**SCENE 4 – Thinking face**
*(Face close-up: son chin on hands, eyes scanning the board. Light tick-tock sound effect.)*
📱 Text: **Plan…**
*(Overhead: he moves his pawn sideways, around the wall.)*
📱 Text: **…find a way…**
*(He places his own wall in front of Mom's pawn – "tock".)*
**Son:** "Ab aap blocked!"
📱 Text: **…outsmart! 😎**

**SCENE 5 – The win**
*(Quick cuts of two last moves. Son's pawn reaches Mom's side. He throws his arms up.)*
**Son:** "Main jeet gaya!"
**Mom:** *(fake-shocked, then claps)* "Arre! Champion of the night!"
📱 Text: **Champion of the night 🏆**

**SCENE 6 – Warm close**
*(Wide: both laughing, son hugs Mom, the board glowing between the diyas.)*
**Mom (VO):** "Is Diwali, screen nahi… dimaag chalao."
📱 Text: **The best Diwali games are played together 💛**

**END CARD (3 sec)**
*(Red Wudly card · Barrier Minds product shot · logo top-right.)*
📱 Text: **Barrier Minds · Strategy game · Rs. 1,399 · wudlytoys.com**

## 12-sec cut-down
Board hero (1s) → "Blocked!" / "Nooo!" (3s) → thinking face + wall drop (3s) → "Main jeet gaya!" (2s) → end card (3s)

## Must-have shots
- Overhead hero shot of the board among diyas, pawns at start (no people)
- Macro: a wall sliding into the groove (slow-mo, clean "tock" sound)
- Son's thinking face – roll for 30–60 sec, keep the best 2 sec
- Son's arms-up win reaction
- Macro of wood grain + Wudly logo on the board corner

## Director tips
- **Explain the rule once off-camera:** move one square, or place one wall; you can't fully close a path.
- Play a real round and keep rolling – but Mom should leave him an easy path round so the win happens on camera.
- The "Blocked!" / "Nooo!" moment is the hook – do 3–4 takes, the funniest reaction wins.
- Fan/AC off for the wooden "tock" sound; add music only in edit.
- LED diyas only near the board; real diyas stay in the background with Mom.
- Signed parent consent before filming.
