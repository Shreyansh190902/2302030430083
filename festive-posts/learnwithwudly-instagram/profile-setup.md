## Posting tips
- Post the reels from `reels/`. They end with the @learnwithwudly outro.
- Upload the matching image from `covers/` as each reel's cover.
- The covers keep the text in the centre, so they look right in the 3:4 profile grid.
- Turn ON "Also share to feed".
- Add the same music as the YouTube video, or keep the original sound.
- Pin EP01 to the top of the profile.
- Story highlights: "Episodes", "Colours", "Games", "Shop" (link to @wudly_toys).
